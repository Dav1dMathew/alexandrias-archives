# Credits

## Landmark models

The four curated landmarks shipped in
`src/main/resources/data/alexandriasarchives/landmarks/` are **community
builds by other authors**, not original work of this project. They were
downloaded from Planet Minecraft and converted to the mod's format by
`tools/convert_landmarks.py`; the geometry is theirs unchanged.

| Landmark | Author | Source | Original format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leaning Tower of Pisa | **The_Fox** | Planet Minecraft | Minecraft Java world save |
| Concorde | **TheOfficialNano** | Planet Minecraft | MCEdit `.schematic` (2014) |
| Eiffel Tower | **tempis335** | Planet Minecraft | Sponge `.schem` |
| Burj Khalifa | **DBYT** | Planet Minecraft | Minecraft Java world save |

> **Attribution is required wherever this mod is distributed or demonstrated.**
> Keep this table intact, and repeat the author names in the submission
> document and in any video that shows these structures being built.
>
> Planet Minecraft submissions are published under each author's own terms.
> Before distributing the mod publicly — or entering it anywhere that counts
> as publication — confirm each author permits redistribution inside another
> project, and seek permission where it is not explicit. Credit alone is not
> automatically a licence.

These models are distinct from the procedural geometry in
`ai-service/generation/archetypes.py`, which is original to this project.
